## History
Characters go back to antiquity in the form of gods, heros and demons --they need to be recognizable and express their natures in a graphic way which can be read by all within the culture

Not all cultures find all characters equally legible, but for a peculiar reason, some of the widest and most recognizable characteristics have to do with "cuteness"

## What is Cute?

We might argue about what makes a character attractive, seem smart or evil, whatever, but it may be that ["cute" is wired into our brains](https://www.scienceabc.com/nature/why-do-we-find-babies-cute.html#:~:text=Konrad%20Lorenz%2C%20an%20Austrian%20ethologist,otherwise%20do%20anything%20for%20themselves.) as a species

Look at Mickey Mouse, Porky Pig and Japanese anime, the "cute" characters all have child-like qualities, notably enormous heads and great big eyes

<figure>
<img src="https://qph.fs.quoracdn.net/main-qimg-3327e70fa95fc5ae8b9bc2e356fb1c77-lq" alt="Mickey Mouse in Steamboat Willie (1928)" style="width:400px;">
  <figcaption><h6>Mickey Mouse is undeniably "cute" and the effect appears to be global</figcaption>
</figure>

Without these qualities, they're not so cute

<figure>
<img src="https://i.redd.it/8lzefntsjba51.png" alt="Porky Pig in varying proportions" style="width:400px;">
  <figcaption><h6>Describe the personality of each in three words</figcaption>
</figure>

Rounded cheeks, bellies, butts help complete the design

Sometimes these proportions can become extreme and the cuteness can become a bit "uncanny" 

<figure>
<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/f/f4/Kamichama_Karin_Volume_1_Japanese.jpg" alt="kamichama" style="width:400px;">

The response to cuteness can have a compulsive element, people can experience a visceral reaction and it's important to carefully and appropriately apply this design element
