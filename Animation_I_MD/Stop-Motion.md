## Stop-Motion

Stop motion animation refers to the trick of stopping a film camera, making a change to a scene, photographing that change and sometimes repeating the process


The "stop trick" was invented by [Georges Méliès](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PWHj1AAyXSU) it's just about the very first "special effect" in cinema!


It sometimes has a sort of comic effect —here it is being used in a poignant satire on war, in 1952s [_Neighbors_](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8z95M_ktpr4)

### How To-torial

There are a number of ways we'll try to create stop-motion animation

1. **Shoot first, ask questions later**  
If you shoot video of [a person "flying" or hovering over the ground](https://youtu.be/OTu7uhJi1ds?t=18), it's going to be too much trouble to try to take individual photos ith that person in the air, so:
* Take video of the person jumping, stepping forward and jumping again
* In Adobe Premier, [export an "image sequence"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r1nWR8t43gY) of ordered jpg or png images 
    * View this sequence on your computer in the largest "icon" (rather than "list") view so you can see the pictures
    * Throw away all unwanted images
 * From this point you can: 
    * [Create a video or gif from your edited image sequence](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gvIpx0BTQTA)

2. **Shoot only What You'll Eat** 

If the action is not too fast to capture individual still images, that may save you a step   
Once you have your "image sequence" everything's the same as above

> **Things to Think About:**  
* You can process the images in the image sequence in photoshop prior to creating your video -paint over them by hand, adding effects etc, or [batch process the images](https://youtu.be/Q1wetbmIyTE?t=49)
* Consider what music or sound you might want to add to your video. For our purposes, use only free or non copyrighted sounds
    * https://freesound.org/ (check the license type and follow the rules! If attribution is required, you'll need to create a title sequence for the end of your video)
    * A great option is [sounds already in the public domain](https://cylinders.library.ucsb.edu/)

> **Think Professionally:** You're probably always going to want to attribute anyone else's work you ever use!
